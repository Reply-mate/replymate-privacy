Reply Mate

Stuck on what to say to a match?

Paste their message, get one warm reply idea, and send it yourself.

A pocket second opinion for your dating chats. It's not a bot that chats for you, there's no account, and it never touches your dating app.

Works with any dating chat you can copy text from.

Android only, in Australia for now. Adults 18+ only.

How it works

  1. Paste your match's message into Reply Mate.
  2. Get one reply idea: short, friendly, usually with an easy question to keep the chat going.
  3. You send it. Copy it, tweak it or ignore it, then send it yourself in your dating app.

Straight talk

  • Not a dating network. No profiles, no matching, no swiping.
  • Never auto-sends. It doesn't connect to your dating app and can't send messages for you. It only asks for internet access.
  • No sign-up. No account, name, email or phone number.
  • Adults 18+ only. Reply Mate is made for grown-up dating and isn't for anyone under 18.

Honest AI

Suggestions are AI-generated (by our AI provider, OpenAI). Every suggestion is just a starting point. If one misses the mark, tap Report in the app so we can improve it.

Built-in safety stops

Reply Mate is designed to stop and show a safety note instead of a reply when a message asks for passwords, bank or card details, verification codes, money or investments, involves someone under 18, or shows signs of crisis or self-harm. No automatic check catches everything, so trust your instincts and use your dating app's report and block tools if something feels off.

Privacy, in plain terms

  • The text you paste goes over an encrypted connection to the Reply Mate service and is processed by OpenAI to create a suggestion.
  • Recent messages are held in temporary memory so suggestions can follow the thread. They aren't written to a database. Our AI provider may keep data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • No ads, no analytics trackers, and we don't sell your data. This page has no trackers either.
  • Tip: leave out full names, addresses and phone numbers when you paste.
