Reply Mate community guidelines

A few simple rules to keep Reply Mate safe and respectful for everyone.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The basics

  • You must be 18 or over. Reply Mate is for adults only.
  • Be respectful and kind. There's a real person on the other side of every chat.
  • No harassment, threats, hate or bullying. Don't use Reply Mate to put anyone down, scare them or target them for who they are.
  • Never anything sexual involving minors. Reply Mate stops on any sign that someone is under 18.
  • No impersonation. Don't pretend to be someone else.
  • No scams. Don't ask anyone for money, or for personal or financial details.

You're in charge of what you send

Reply Mate only suggests a reply. It never sends anything for you. You choose what to send, so you're responsible for it. Read each suggestion, and change it or skip it if it doesn't feel right.

If something goes wrong

  • If someone makes you feel unsafe, use your dating app's report and block tools.
  • In a crisis, call Lifeline on 13 11 14 (24 hours, 7 days). If you're in immediate danger, call 000.
  • If a suggestion misses the mark, tap Report on it in the app so we can improve it.

If you break these guidelines

We may block suggestions for your install, limit or remove your access to Reply Mate, and report illegal content (especially anything involving minors) to the authorities.

You can report any suggestion in the app. Tap Report on it.
