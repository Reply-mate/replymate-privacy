Reply Mate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026 (AEST)
Contact: info@savysaver.com.au

Who we are

Reply Mate (“we”) provides a companion mobile app that helps adults draft replies for dating chats on apps like Bumble, Hinge, and Tinder. You paste a message into Reply Mate, get a suggestion, and you copy and send it yourself. We do not operate a dating network and we do not send messages into those apps for you.

What we collect

  • Messages you paste or type in the app (user-generated content), sent to our server so we can generate a reply suggestion.
  • Conversation ID — a random UUID stored on your device and sent with requests so suggestions can stay coherent in a thread.
  • Bearer token — stored only on your device (encrypted preferences) to authenticate to our API. Not a Google login in v0.
  • Technical basics our host may see (IP, timestamps) as part of normal HTTPS hosting.

We do not collect contacts, photos, precise location, or SMS in v0.

How we use data

  • App functionality: generate one suggested reply per submission.
  • Safety: hard stops for crisis/self-harm cues and anything involving minors (no dating suggestion; safety handoff).
  • We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising in v0.

Sharing

  • Our server (currently hosted on Render) processes requests.
  • OpenAI (or another LLM provider we configure) may receive prompt text derived from your pasted messages to generate the suggestion. They act as a service provider for that purpose.
  • We do not share data to post into Bumble, Hinge, or Tinder.

Retention

On device: conversation ID and token until you clear app data or reset in Settings. On server: short-lived conversation memory for active threads; we do not run a long-term marketing profile on your chats in v0. Hosting/provider logs follow their retention.

Security

HTTPS in transit. Token in EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android. No method is perfect — protect your device and do not share your Bearer token.

Children

Reply Mate is for adults 18+ only. We do not knowingly provide dating-reply help involving minors.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including Australia), you may ask what we hold, ask for deletion of server-side thread data we can locate, or withdraw use by stopping use and clearing the app. Email the contact above.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Material Play-related changes will be reflected before or with the relevant app update.
